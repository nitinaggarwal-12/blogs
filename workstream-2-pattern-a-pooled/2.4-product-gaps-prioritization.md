## 2. Joint Engineering Sign-Off Criteria (Milestone 1 Gate)

1. **Zero Forged Header Passthrough**: 100% of client-supplied `X-Tenant-ID` headers are stripped at Hop 1 before reaching ADK callbacks.
2. **Deterministic Tool & Agent Pruning**: RetailStream receives HTTP `403 Forbidden` when calling FinVault's `Agent Alpha` directly by URI or attempting to invoke FinVault's Google Workspace / Jira MCP servers.
3. **Tiered Thinking & Rate Bulkheads**: RetailStream requests exceeding `4,000` thinking tokens are automatically clamped to `4,000`, and bursts above `120 RPM` are rejected with `429 Too Many Requests` without impacting FinVault's `8,000`-token / `600 RPM` lane.
4. **Tenant-Aware PII Redaction & RLS**: FinVault stack traces have bank account (`ACCT-...`) and SWIFT codes redacted while RetailStream logs have credit card PANs redacted; AlloyDB RLS (`SET LOCAL app.current_tenant`) prevents cross-tenant row reads even under simulated SQL filter tampering.
