2. Key Production Trade-offs & Isolation Guarantees

HARDWARE & PERIMETER
01
Zero-Shared Infrastructure: Dedicated per-tenant GCP projects, private VPC Service Controls (VPC-SC) perimeters, and isolated GKE node pools eliminate co-tenancy risk.
02

03
Cross-Project IAM Confinement: Prohibits cross-tenant service account impersonation via Organization IAM v3 Principal Access Boundaries (PAB).
